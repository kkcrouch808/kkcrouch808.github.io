# My Eye-Opening Experience Using AI in ICS 314

---
---

## Introduction

Artificial intelligence has become a common tool in education, especially in technical fields like software engineering. In ICS 314, AI tools were not only allowed but encouraged when used responsibly and transparently. The course emphasized learning how to use AI as a support tool rather than a shortcut for avoiding effort or understanding.

Throughout the semester, I made frequent use of AI tools such as GitHub Copilot and ChatGPT. These tools supported my work across WODs, essays, learning new concepts, and the final project. Using AI helped me work more efficiently and understand complex topics, but it also required careful judgment to avoid over-reliance.

---

## Personal Experience with AI

### Experience WODs

In the beginning, I used ChatGPT to assist me with the WODs. I would put the WOD instructions straight into ChatGPT along with any additional information I had. It helped me get through some WODs, but it was hard to fix areas that were not working correctly. I then learned how to use GitHub Copilot, which was much more efficient. When I needed very specific things corrected, Copilot would sometimes struggle and I would go in circles trying to rephrase my prompt. I learned that it was often easier to fix issues myself or look at previous examples. My Copilot prompts usually included the full WOD instructions with any extra notes.

---

---

### In-class WODs

For in-class WODs, I again transitioned from ChatGPT to Copilot. ChatGPT was useful for understanding instructions, but Copilot worked better when writing code directly in the editor with project context. I often included test cases or edge cases in my prompts. When AI responses were incomplete or incorrect, I corrected the code myself to stay within the time limit.

---

### Essays

I used ChatGPT to support the writing of my essays. AI helped me with: Helping me refine my thoughts and points on the examples, so that my explanations were clearer and more complete. Providing editorial feedback on structure, clarity, and tone. The AI tools supported me in brainstorming, drafting, and polishing my work. An example prompt would include the essay instructions, the technical essay guide, any thoughts or additional information needed and a simple prompt: “based off of this information, make a draft for my essay”, “does my essay follow the requirements and instructions”, “make my essay sound more professional” 

---

### Final Project

For my final project, I used mainly ChatGPT and CoPilot, although I experimented with AntiGravity and found it to be too new to be useful. CoPilot is very good at working on websites when you use it correctly and found myself using it heavily for maximum efficiency. I used ChatGPT to explain concepts and errors to me and had it answer any quick questions I had. However, sometimes my questions were too specific to my project and CoPilot would do a great job answering them. When working on Mānoa RoomieMatch, I would make CoPilot draft what I needed first, then work on small, specific tasks. Sometimes I would ask it for input and go back and forth with CoPilot to figure out the best way of going about a task. It usually had great ideas and gave multiple ways of doing tasks and once I liked a plan it was easy to ask it to complete it for me. Example of CoPilot prompt: 
- “I would like to update the "View profile" it seems like it is not fully accurate. I removed the smoking, drinking, and pets a while ago but it still seems to show up across the website like in the filter tool too. I need those removed everywhere. Also, the interests are currently showing green boxes with no text. Let me know if you neeed clarification”
- “Currently, when a user makes a new account, they are prompted with the lifestyle survey to complete. After the new user completes the survey, I want the user to be prompted with the edit profile page. Do you have any other suggestions”
- "What do you think about adding a filter and sort tool to browse matches page? what suggestions do you have? I feel that sorting by gender and dorm prefrence would be most useful"

### Learning a Concept or Tutorial

For learning concepts, I mainly used ChatGPT. ChatGPT is great at explaining basic concepts and I could even customize how I wanted the explanation. If the explanation was too complex for me to understand, I could simply add a “explain this to me like I’m 15” or “add analogies” or “explain in one paragraph using very simple terms”. I could also ask questions about these concepts like it was my tutor. I found that tutorials are best on YouTube unless it's something simple like terminal commands.

---

### Answering Questions in Class or Discord

Most of my questions in class or on Discord were related to clarification of assignment instructions. I did not usually use AI for this because I felt comfortable asking questions directly and did not need help phrasing them.

---

### Asking or Answering a Smart Question

I did not ask or answer smart questions during this course.

### Coding Example

I do not recall needing AI-generated coding examples in ICS 314.

### Explaining Code

I would ask ChatGPT and CoPilot to explain the code. I would mainly use Chat GPT unless it required more context of the project as a whole. I found that both are great at explaining code. If the explanation was too complex for me to understand, I could simply add a “explain this to me like I’m 15” or “add analogies” or “explain in one paragraph using very simple terms”. I could also ask questions about these concepts like it was my tutor.

---

### Writing Code

I used GitHub Copilot extensively when writing code. Copilot helped speed up development by generating boilerplate code, suggesting functions, and completing repetitive patterns. I reviewed and edited all generated code to ensure correctness and understanding. A good example of a prompt I used in my final project included my whole proposal essay [*see here*](https://kkcrouch808.github.io/essays/final-project.html) followed by the mockup image of the landing page and "create a draft landing paged based off of this information and my mockup image".

---

### Documenting Code

I mainly used CoPilot to document my code, it was very effective at doing so. I would add “add comments explaining” to the end of some of my prompts. Or after I completed one of my issues, I would send a prompt like “add comments to everything”.

---

### Quality Assurance

When fixing bugs or ESLint errors, CoPilot would usually double-check for me before I could even ask. Before I started using CoPilot, I would paste my errors into ChatGPT and any context it needed to fix my code. In the beginning the ESLint errors gave me a lot of trouble especially because CoPilot wasn't able to see them. I larned to fix errors on my own and if I needed extra help from CoPilot I learned I could refrence my terminal to have it fix the errors for me. An example CoPilot prompt for fixing ESLint errors: "Run the commad to check for ESLint errors, if there are any, fix them" or after running "npm run lint" I would say "fix my lint errors, see terminal".

---

### Other Uses

I also used AI for brainstorming UI ideas, improving consistency across pages, and thinking through user experience decisions. For example, our team used ChatGPT to come up with mockup pages before we started coding. Here is a prompt I used for our resources page: "Can you help me make a mockup page for our resources page for our roomate matching website? I'm going to include mostly school housing resources but I'd like to include information on each site and pages of the websites. It should be green themed. make a mockup image".

## Impact on Learning and Understanding

AI had a strong impact on how I learned in ICS 314. It helped me understand difficult concepts more quickly and reduced frustration when I got stuck. Being able to ask follow-up questions or request simpler explanations improved my confidence and comprehension.

However, I also learned that relying too heavily on AI could limit understanding. Over time, I became more intentional about using AI as a learning tool rather than a solution generator.

---

## Practical Applications

AI was especially useful during the final project, where real-world complexity made development more challenging. AI helped me plan UI changes, think through user flows, and identify edge cases. While it did not solve problems on its own, it made the development process more efficient.

---

## Challenges and Opportunities

One challenge was learning how to write effective prompts. Vague prompts led to unhelpful answers, while detailed prompts required more effort. This pushed me to improve my prompt-writing skills and better understand AI’s limitations.

---

## Comparative Analysis

Compared to traditional learning methods, AI-enhanced learning offered faster feedback and more flexibility. My experience using AI in ICS 314 was extremely eye-opening. I’m used to having to follow strict guidelines with my AI usage in all of my classes. Having complete freedom with how I use AI was a game changer. It allowed me to experiment, make mistakes, and learn how to use AI as a real support tool rather than something I was afraid to touch or rely on too much. In this class, I learned how to use AI effectively and gained skills tha I will be able to apply to real world problems and future jobs.

---

## Future Considerations

AI will likely continue to play a larger role in software engineering education. Clear guidelines and responsible use will be important to ensure students still develop strong foundational skills.

---

## Conclusion

Overall, AI played an important role in my experience in ICS 314. When used thoughtfully, it improved my learning and efficiency without replacing independent thinking. Balancing AI use with hands-on practice helped prepare me for future software engineering work.
